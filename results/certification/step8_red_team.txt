ATTACK 1: 'The 11-of-22 disagreement is an artefact of borderline effects crossing an alpha threshold.'
==============================================================================================================
Refutation, from raw data:
  1. The claim does not rest on the threshold. Testing the seeds against EACH OTHER (no alpha on the effect at all): Friedman test significant in 13 of 22 cells,
     permutation test in 10; after Holm over 22 cells: Friedman 8, permutation 7  (results/certification/step1_disagreement.txt:58-59)
     cells that survive Holm on the permutation p (7): ['DAVIS XAttn-Ref random', 'KIBA HyperAttentionDTI random', 'KIBA MolTrans random', 'DAVIS HyperAttentionDTI cold_drug', 'KIBA MolTrans cold_drug', 'DAVIS XAttn-Ref cold_drug', 'KIBA XAttn-Ref random']
  2. The variation is training, not scoring: re-collecting the explanation from the same checkpoint moves P@10 by sd 0.0008; between-seed sd is 0.0121 (15.5x larger) (step1c)
  3. Seed ORDER is stable across two independent collection passes in 21 of 22 cells (step1c): the seeds really do differ from one another.
  4. Effect size, not just significance: HyperAttentionDTI DAVIS cold-drug seeds 0.0249 / 0.0768 / 0.0195 vs chance 0.0204: best/worst = 3.94x; between-seed sd 0.032 vs protein-sampling SE 0.0018 (step2).
  5. The spread exceeds the distance from chance in 21 of 22 cells (step1) -- reported, but the draft itself calls it weak near chance.
What the data do NOT let us say (concede these):
  - 'Exactly 12'. The sampled 1,000-permutation run gave 12; the exact test gives 11; the count is 8 at alpha 0.01 and 13 at 0.10. It is a threshold-dependent count.
  - The direct test finds significant seed variance in 7-8 of 22 cells, i.e. in about a third, not in most cells. 'Verdicts are unstable' is supported; 'every cell is unstable' is not.
  VERDICT: attack answered; the paper must lead with the direct test and the effect sizes, not with the threshold count.

==============================================================================================================
ATTACK 2: 'The models learned nothing; the attention is noise, so the audit audits noise.'
==============================================================================================================
Refutation, from raw data:
  1. The models predict binding: DAVIS random-split test AUROC of the five models 0.891-0.937 (sklearn on raw predictions, step5c); a noise model scores 0.5.
  2. The attention is used by the model: masking the attended residues beats size-matched random masking with a lower bound above zero in 16 of 16 cells (three models, seeds and targets resampled, step5b).
  3. The weights carry site information even where the attention does not show it: integrated gradients on XAttn-Ref, pocket enrichment above chance at 4 of 4 DAVIS levels;
     cold-drug 3.16 [2.40, 4.38] against attention 2.10 (independent recomputation; draft 3.16 [2.40, 4.40]).
  4. The pipeline can see a real signal: attention pocket enrichment survives in XAttn-Ref at 4 of 4 and HyperAttentionDTI at 3 of 4 DAVIS levels (step5a), and the positive control detects a 0.02 dose at every level (results/positive_control_davis.md).
  5. The saved numbers are the network's numbers: live forward passes on the GPU reproduce the saved logits within 1e-5 for 40 of 40 pairs, with identical checkpoint hashes (step7).
What the data do NOT let us say (concede these):
  - IG is a confirmatory, partial result: against UniProt residues only 1 of 4 XAttn-Ref levels are above chance ([0.61, 1.59, 0.99, 0.19]); HyperAttentionDTI's KIBA cold-drug IG interval [0.64, 3.45] includes parity.
  - Above-chance pocket enrichment is coarse (no conservation control): it does not show the models learned binding chemistry, only that they do not ignore the pocket.
  - The strongest claim the data support is 'the models are not noise and their weights hold pocket information that attention under-reports', not 'they learned binding sites'.
  VERDICT: attack answered by accuracy + faithfulness + IG; the 3.16x figure alone would not be enough and must not be presented as the shield.

==============================================================================================================
ATTACK 3: 'The DAVIS leak invalidates the whole benchmark.'
==============================================================================================================
Refutation, from raw data:
  1. cold_target: 12 test targets are seen by sequence; 0 of them are scored by any of the four attention models' explanation analyses (68 distinct proteins scored, 3 seeds x 4 models). The explanation results never touch the leak.
  1. cold_pair: 11 test targets are seen by sequence; 0 of them are scored by any of the four attention models' explanation analyses (72 distinct proteins scored, 3 seeds x 4 models). The explanation results never touch the leak.
  2. The leak is measured and bounded: it affects 12 of 88 cold-target test targets (816 of 5,984 rows, 13.6%) and 11 of 88 cold-pair targets (143 of 1,144 rows) (step3, sequence audit recomputed from the split CSVs).
  3. On the leaked rows the sequence match inflates DeepDTA AUROC by 0.116 (95% t-interval [0.098, 0.134], p = 0.0013); on the unleaked rows -0.019 (p = 0.31): accuracy on sequence-unseen targets is not inflated.
  4. The signature is present in all four models (leaked minus unleaked AUROC, cold-target): DeepDTA +0.066, MolTrans +0.117, HyperAttentionDTI +0.061, XAttn-Ref +0.060; the paper reports cold-level accuracy on sequence-unseen targets and states the correction.
  5. The headline (seed instability, calibrated battery, readout dependence) is built on per-protein explanation scores at all four levels, including DAVIS random and cold-drug, where every test target is also in the training set by construction, so the cold-start leak cannot arise.
What the data do NOT let us say (concede these):
  - The retrain is DeepDTA at cold-target only, three seeds. All-rows effect +0.019, CI [-0.022, 0.059], p = 0.19: not distinguishable from zero. At cold-pair the effect is -0.021 (p = 0.10) and the smaller training set costs more (0.062).
  - DAVIS cold-pair validation still contains seen-by-sequence targets (checkpoint selection may have been influenced; problem-log row s): open, cannot be fixed without retraining.
  VERDICT: attack answered for the explanation results; the accuracy claims at cold levels carry a disclosed, partly unquantified leak.

==============================================================================================================
ATTACK 4 (added): 'Three seeds cannot support any of this; your intervals are an artefact of resampling three numbers.'
==============================================================================================================
Refutation, from raw data:
  1. The intervals are conservative, and the paper says so: with 3 seeds, 1 in 9 resamples draws one seed three times. The headline cell's two-way interval is stable across 10 RNG seeds (lower 0.926-0.942, upper 3.629-3.710) and crosses parity in every run.
  2. The residue-level verdict is robust to the aggregation and to the family: the single survivor (HyperAttentionDTI, DAVIS random) is also the single Holm survivor under Holm on 16 cells, Bonferroni and Benjamini-Hochberg (step4), and its two-way lower bound is 1.32 (step2).
  3. The claims that need many seeds are the ones we do NOT make: no cell's verdict depends on a lower bound within 0.03 of 1 at the residue level (step2), and the borderline pocket cell (HyperAttentionDTI cold-pair, lower bound 1.02) is labelled marginal on slide and in the draft.
What the data do NOT let us say (concede these):
  - Per-seed Holm keeps 5 of 48 DAVIS seed-runs and 3 of 18 KIBA seed-runs: the 'only 1 of 20' statement is about the median-over-seeds rule, and is stated as such.
  - Seeds 4 and 5 (Wave A) are planned, not run: no number in the paper depends on them; they may sharpen the instability counts but cannot remove the disagreements already observed.
  VERDICT: attack partly stands (3 seeds is small); the paper's wording already scopes claims to what 3 seeds can support.
